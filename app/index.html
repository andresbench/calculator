Andre’s Bench — damage calculator and rotation track for Arknights: Endfield

The build editor shows an operator’s stats with weapon, gear, potentials and talents, and the damage of every hit with the formula broken down factor by factor. The rotation track plays a whole team’s rotation in time: SP, ultimate energy, cooldowns, combo triggers, debuffs, stagger and buffs, and adds up the damage and DPS. Builds can be imported from the in-game showcase through Enka.Network.

Every number is a fact from the game data or a measurement, a named assumption, or listed as not counted. Free, open source (MIT), in English and Russian. Unofficial fan project.
